Security & Encryption

End-to-End Encryption

Your sensitive data is protected with military-grade encryption before it ever leaves your device.

  • Master Password: Your encryption key that never leaves your device
  • PBKDF2 Key Derivation: 200,000 iterations with SHA256 hashing
  • AES Encryption: Industry-standard symmetric encryption
  • Session Security: Temporary key storage with automatic cleanup
  • Zero-Knowledge Architecture: Even administrators cannot access your data

Two-Factor Authentication

Enhanced account security with multiple verification methods.

  • TOTP Authenticator: Compatible with Google Authenticator, Authy, and similar apps
  • Email Verification: Backup 2FA method via email codes
  • Recovery Codes: One-time backup codes for emergency access
  • Session Management: Control active sessions across devices

Account Recovery

Secure recovery options while maintaining encryption integrity.

  • Recovery Keys: 32-character keys for data recovery
  • Key Regeneration: Create new recovery keys when needed
  • Secure Storage: Offline storage recommendations
